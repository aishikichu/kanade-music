🔬 How to verify True Hi-Res from the Spectrogram:
👑 True Hi-Res Master (24-bit / 48-96+ kHz):
Acoustic energy and harmonics extend continuously above 20 kHz and up to 24 kHz+ with no artificial brick-wall cutoff.
🎧 CD Lossless (16-bit / 44.1 kHz):
Natural frequency response up to the Red Book CD ceiling at 22.05 kHz.
⚡ Upsampled / Lossy Source Detection: If the graph shows a hard horizontal shelf/cutoff abruptly cut off at 16 kHz or 19 kHz with blank black space above it, the audio originated from a lossy compressed source prior to FLAC encoding.
